How Astrology Works

Astrology is an ancient practice that studies the positions and movements of celestial bodies to gain insight into human affairs and earthly events. Your zodiac sign (also called sun sign) is determined by the position of the Sun at the time of your birth. Each sign is associated with specific personality traits, elemental energies (fire, earth, air, water), and ruling planets. Daily horoscopes analyze the current planetary positions relative to your sign to generate guidance for the day.

Zodiac Compatibility

Wondering if you're compatible with someone? Check both your sign and theirs! Generally, signs of the same element get along well: Fire signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) share passion and energy; Earth signs (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn) value stability and practicality; Air signs (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) connect through intellect and communication; Water signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) bond through emotion and intuition. Opposite signs often attract, creating dynamic but challenging relationships.

FAQ

When should I check my horoscope? — Check it each morning to set the tone for your day. The forecast updates at midnight UTC.

Can I read other signs' horoscopes? — Absolutely! Many people read their partner's, friend's, or child's sign for relationship insights.

What if I was born on a cusp? — If your birthday falls on the boundary between two signs, you may resonate with both. Read both horoscopes and see which feels more accurate.

Are horoscopes scientifically proven? — No. Astrology is considered a pseudoscience. Treat horoscopes as entertainment and daily reflection, not as factual predictions.
